Our Priorities

Eight proposed priorities for Ikolomani. Each links to a manifesto chapter that explains the concern, the proposal and the limits of an MP’s role.

01

Land protection and community interests

Fair process and clear information for families affected by land decisions.

02

Artisanal miner safety and dignity

Safer working conditions and respect for lawful miners.

03

Responsible mining and environmental stewardship

Protect water, rehabilitate land, address unsafe shafts.

04

Youth skills, enterprise and employment

Practical routes into skills, business and jobs.

05

Education and access to opportunity

Schools that are safe, equipped and within reach.

06

Healthcare and community wellbeing

Closer, better-run care for every ward.

07

Roads, infrastructure and essential services

Roads, water and services families can rely on.

08

Transparent representation and accountability

Regular public reporting and an open record.

Your Voice, Our Accountability

How the campaign proposes to report back.

  1. Concerns receivedAnonymised summaries by ward and topic, published once real submissions have been collected and reviewed.
  2. Consultations heldThe date, place and ward of each public meeting, with a short report.
  3. Public commitmentsA priority becomes a commitment only once it is approved and dated. Its status is then updated with evidence.
  4. Statements and progressOfficial statements published in full, with dates.
