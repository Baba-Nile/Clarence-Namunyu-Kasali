Share a concern. Shape a priority.

Tell the campaign what matters in your ward. Concerns help decide which priorities are researched first.

Ward concern form

This form is not connected to a delivery service yet. Nothing you send here will reach the campaign until the team finishes setup. You can still try the form; it will tell you clearly that nothing was sent.

Describe what is happening, where, and what you would like done. Please do not include other people’s names or personal details.

Only give a phone number or email if you would like a reply. You can send a concern anonymously.

We ask only for what we need. Individual messages are not published.

What happens to your message

  • The campaign team reads it. It is not published.
  • Concerns are grouped by ward and topic to decide what is researched first.
  • A reply is possible only if you share a phone number or email.
  • This form is not for emergencies. For urgent safety problems, contact the emergency services or the responsible authority.
