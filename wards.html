Know Your Ward

Ikolomani has four electoral wards. See each ward’s reported 2022 electorate and the topics the campaign proposes to research with residents.

Figures are IEBC’s registered voters for the 2022 general election, checked 9 October 2026. They are historical, not the 2027 electorate, and show the size of an electorate rather than how severe a ward’s challenges are. Research priorities are topics to investigate, not findings.
