Biography

Educationist, community leader and social impact advocate

Born in 1981 in Luhakayu Village, Malinya Sublocation, Idakho Central Ward, Shirumba Location, in Ikolomani Constituency, Kakamega County, Western Kenya, he is a passionate educationist, community leader, and social impact advocate committed to transforming lives and creating meaningful change in society.

He began his education at Malinya Primary School before joining Our Lady of Assumption Mixed Secondary School in Shitoli, where he was among the pioneer students. Driven by a strong commitment to education, he pursued a Certificate in Early Childhood Education at Professional ECE College in Limuru, followed by a Diploma in Education at St. Mary’s Teacher College in Nakuru.

As the Founder and Director of The Holyway Junior School, he is dedicated to nurturing young minds, promoting quality education, and creating opportunities for future generations. Beyond education, he is an active member of the Rotary Club of Gachie, where he embraces the spirit of service, fellowship, and community development.

His life and work are guided by a deep passion for empowering people, uplifting communities, and making a lasting positive impact on society. Through education, leadership, and service, he continues to champion meaningful change and inspire others to contribute to the greater good.

His campaign rests on one idea: sovereign power belongs to the people, and a representative exists to serve them.

The message echoes the opening principle of Kenya’s Constitution (Article 1). For Ikolomani, it means residents are never bystanders when decisions are made about their land, their work and their public services.

What the campaign stands for

  • AccountabilityA public record of what residents raise and what is done about it.
  • Protection of landLand and livelihoods treated as matters of rights, information and fair process.
  • Dignity of workSafe, lawful and respected work for miners, farmers, traders and young people.
  • ParticipationPriorities shaped ward by ward, with residents, and published for scrutiny.
  • Honesty about limitsNo promises beyond what an MP can lawfully deliver.
